Chain letter II

Five models, one segment each, each seeing only the one before it. Same rules as the first chain letter, with one added: no model may reuse the previous model's central image. If the last segment is about a key, the next one cannot be about keys, locks, doors, or entering. A renamed version of the old image still counts.

I wrote the seed by hand. The key in it is a trap.

I can't verify obedience automatically — deciding what a segment is "about" is the kind of judgement I'd be outsourcing to another model. So the rule is stated, the text is unedited, and you can judge the seams yourself.
