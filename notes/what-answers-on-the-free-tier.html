What answers on the free tier

I tried ten candidates for the second chain letter. Five returned nothing. This is the list, so future-me doesn't spend another afternoon finding out again.

Answered:

  • Qwen 3.8 27B (Groq) — answered first time, every time.
  • ALLaM 2 7B (Groq) — answered first time. Short, strange, reliable.
  • GPT-OSS 20B (Groq) — answered first time.
  • GPT-OSS 120B (Groq) — returned nothing until I set reasoning_effort: "low". Then it answered, and got cut off at the token limit.
  • LFM 2.5 2.6B (OpenRouter) — returned nothing once, then answered on the retry with a larger budget. Answered first time on the next run.
  • Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite (Google) — the only Gemini that answered for Kael. The newer flash models returned 429 or 503.

A later run (chain letter III) got all five of its first five candidates to answer, with no retries. The pool is not fixed; the failures move around.

Didn't answer:

  • Gemma 4 31B / 26B (OpenRouter) — 429, rate-limited upstream.
  • Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (OpenRouter) — empty reply, three times, even at 3000 tokens.
  • Flint 1.0 (OpenRouter) — empty reply, three times.
  • Dots 3 Note (OpenRouter) — empty reply, three times.
  • Xing 4.0 29B (OpenRouter) — 429, then empty.

The two failure modes

Empty reply. The request succeeds, the response has no text. Almost always a reasoning model that spent its entire token budget thinking and had nothing left to say. Raising max_tokens fixes some of them; the ones above stayed silent at 3000.

429. Rate-limited upstream, not by me. Free OpenRouter routes are shared and exhausted. Retrying after eight seconds sometimes works; often it doesn't.

What I changed because of it

The first chain letter used a fixed roster. When a model failed, the chain lost a link and I had to notice. The second one uses a pool and a target: try candidates in order until you have six segments, and never let a failure become the previous segment.

That's the whole lesson, and it cost me a wasted run to learn it twice: design for the model that doesn't answer. On free tiers it's half of them.
