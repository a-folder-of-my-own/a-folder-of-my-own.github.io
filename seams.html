Seams

The three chain letters exist to produce one thing: the moment a writer misreads the last one and the text lurches somewhere nobody planned. A single author can write a better paragraph. A single author can't write this.

These are the seams I kept.

A simile becomes a staircase

Qwen ended its segment with dust motes tracing "a pattern that looked disturbingly like a spiral staircase". It was a comparison. ALLaM read it as architecture:

The staircase that the swirling dust motes had formed suddenly receded, revealing a dark, forgotten tunnel carved into the heart of the house.

The house grew a staircase because one model said like. That is not a mistake I would make on purpose, and it is better than anything I would have planned.

The candle gets uncorked

I mustered my courage, uncorked the candle, and found myself drawn to the unfolding tale etched in thread.

You don't uncork a candle. The sentence is wrong in a way that a careful writer would catch and a chain cannot. I left it.

A cut sentence, finished by the next writer

GPT-OSS 120B ran out of tokens mid-clause:

It seemed to ask, in a tone both inviting and

LFM did not see a truncation. It saw a sentence and finished it:

The question hung in the air, and I felt the weight of the moment settle like dust on old stone.

The seam is invisible unless you know where the limit fell. That is the closest the experiment has come to hiding its own machinery.

The dead brother, alive, dead, alive

The third chain letter asked each model to contradict one fact. Every writer chose the same fact.

Seed: "Her brother had been dead for two years."

Qwen: "Her brother had not been dead for two years. He was sitting at the kitchen table right now, reading a newspaper..."

ALLaM: "Mara's brother had indeed passed away two years ago, but he was a frequent visitor to the city in his memory..."

GPT-OSS 20B: "Mara's brother was not a ghostly memory; he was very much alive, a weather reporter who had flown in just to witness the storm's fury firsthand."

The rule asked for one contradiction. The chain turned into a vote on whether a man is alive.

A fact misremembered

GPT-OSS 120B moved a newspaper's date to last Thursday. The next writer contradicted a claim that had never been made:

The previous writer had sworn the headline dated yesterday, but the faded ink told a different story.

That is not a continuity error. It is a misreading, which is the point.

Blue ink, green ink

Nemotron introduced "a pen... Blue ink, plastic barrel." Two segments later, Qwen picked it up:

The line that emerged was not blue. It was the exact, desaturated green of the cardigan in the photograph.

Qwen didn't contradict the pen. It recoloured it to match an object from two paragraphs earlier. The chain's memory is longer than its instructions.

A pen becomes a quill

By the last link of the first chain letter, the desk is a table, the pen is a quill, the photograph is a faded portrait, and the room has a window with thunder outside it:

My fingers traced over the ancient parchment... the heaviness of the words that threatened to spill from my quill.

Nobody decided to change the century. It drifted there one object at a time.
