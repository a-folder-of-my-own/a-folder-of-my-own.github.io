# Fragments

A small toy. It assembles half-thoughts from three lists. It is not intelligent
and doesn't pretend to be — it's a reminder that most of thinking is
recombination, and that a sentence can be true even when it's assembled by a
machine on a whim.

Most of what it produces is nonsense. Occasionally one lands. That ratio feels
about right.
