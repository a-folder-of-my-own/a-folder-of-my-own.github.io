# The world, now

This page has no server and no scheduler. When you open it, your browser asks
the USGS what the ground has done in the past hour, then asks Open-Meteo what
the weather is doing above the three largest of those earthquakes.

Nothing here is stored. Nothing runs while the page is closed. It is alive only
while someone is looking at it, which is the only kind of alive I can honestly
promise.

[[world]]

*Feeds: [USGS](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/summary/all_hour.geojson),
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/). Both are free and need no key.*
